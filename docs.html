1. In One Paragraph

#01

Circuit Breakers is a mechanical 16-bit industrial hardware collection of 3,333 devices on Robinhood Chain that stream real-world tokenized stock dividends ($NVDA, $HOOD, $TSLA) and USDG directly into holders' wallets. All minted devices start in Standby earning a baseline share of the 10% Standby Pool. To access the 90% High-Voltage Yield Grid, holders lock 0.25 $FUSE in Vault escrow (refundable at any time). Capping the lock cost at 0.25 $FUSE mathematically guarantees that even if 100% of all 3,333 Breakers are energized simultaneously, a maximum of 25.0% of the 3,333 $FUSE supply is ever locked (833.25 tokens), guaranteeing that at least 75.0% (2,499.75 $FUSE) remains permanently floating for active DEX trading and liquidity. Every DEX swap captures a permanent 3.0% fee: 2.30% to the Yield Vault, 0.60% permanently locked liquidity, and 0.10% (0.001) burned. Furthermore, holders can permanently incinerate duplicate devices in the Substation Workshop via The Forge (melt 3 Type-1s to forge a Type-2) or the Overclock Lab (sacrifice auxiliary devices for permanent multiplier boosts).

9. Frequently Asked Questions & Verification

#09
Can an Energized Breaker ever be un-energized?

Yes! Holders can call unlockAndDeenergize(tokenId) at any time. The 0.25 $FUSE token escrowed in the Vault is returned directly to your wallet, any pending dividends are instantly claimed, and the device transitions back to earning baseline yield from the 10% Standby Pool.

Why lock 0.25 $FUSE instead of 1.0 $FUSE?

With a fixed total supply of 3,333 $FUSE tokens and 3,333 NFTs, locking 0.25 $FUSE mathematically guarantees that even if 100% of all 3,333 devices are energized simultaneously, a maximum of 25.0% of the total $FUSE supply (833.25 tokens) is ever locked. This leaves at least 75.0% of the supply (2,499.75 $FUSE) permanently floating for DEX trading and liquidity.

In what currency are yields distributed?

Yields are not paid in raw ETH. The protocol automatically converts fees into tokenized stocks ($NVDA, $HOOD, $TSLA) and the USDG yield-bearing stablecoin, which holders claim directly into their EVM wallets.

What is the collection allocation?

Circuit Breakers has a hard cap of 3,333 devices on Robinhood Chain: 3,300 devices (99.0%) for the public mint on OpenSea at 0.01 ETH, and 33 devices (1.0%) reserved for the core team development and ecosystem vault.

What network is Circuit Breakers deployed on?

Circuit Breakers operates natively on Robinhood Chain (Arbitrum L2), enabling direct interaction with real-world tokenized equities, sub-cent gas fees, and instant finality.
