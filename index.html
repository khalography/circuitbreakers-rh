FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about the Circuit Breakers protocol at a glance.

What is Circuit Breakers?

Circuit Breakers is a collection of 3,333 mechanical 16-bit industrial devices on Robinhood Chain that stream real-world tokenized stock dividends ($NVDA, $HOOD, $TSLA, US T-Bills) directly into holders' EVM wallets.

How much is the mint and where do I mint?

The mint price is 0.01 ETH per breaker on OpenSea. The total supply is hard-capped at 3,333 devices with zero future dilution.

How do I energize my breaker?

Upon minting, your breaker is in a Tripped (Unlit) state. To start streaming dividends, you connect to the Yield Desk and burn 1.0 $FUSE token. This permanently snaps your breaker's knife switch shut [ON], locking it into the high-voltage yield grid.

Where does the stock yield revenue come from?

A 2.0% volume fee from every $FUSE swap across Robinhood Chain DEX pairs is routed into the Vault and distributed to Energized Breakers proportionally to their archetype multiplier weights.

Is there a team allocation or private pre-sale?

No. Circuit Breakers operates under a 100% Community Fair Launch with 0% team allocation, 0% advisor reserve, and 0% venture capital backing.
