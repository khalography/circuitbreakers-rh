FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about the Circuit Breakers protocol at a glance.

What is Circuit Breakers?

Circuit Breakers is a collection of 3,333 mechanical 16-bit industrial devices on Robinhood Chain that stream real-world tokenized stock dividends ($NVDA, $HOOD, $TSLA) and the $USDG stablecoin directly into holders' EVM wallets.

How much is the mint and where do I mint?

The mint price is 0.01 ETH per breaker on OpenSea. The total supply is hard-capped at 3,333 devices (3,300 Public / 33 Team).

How does the Lock-to-Energize & 90/10 Yield Split work?

All Breakers start in a Standby state earning continuous dividends from the 10% Standby Pool. To access the 90% High-Voltage Yield Grid, connect to the Yield Desk and lock 0.25 $FUSE token in Vault escrow. Even if all 3,333 devices are energized, a maximum of 25% of the total $FUSE supply is ever locked (preserving 75%+ float for DEX liquidity). Your tokens are not burned and can be unlocked at any time.

What are the Dual NFT Burn mechanics in the Workshop?

Holders can permanently burn secondary Breakers in the Substation Workshop: The Forge allows melting 3 Type-1 devices to forge 1 rare Type-2 device (1.5× multiplier). The Overclock Lab allows sacrificing auxiliary devices to permanently supercharge a Master Breaker's yield multiplier (+0.25× per Type-1, +0.50× per Type-2).

Where does the stock yield revenue come from?

A 2.30% volume fee from every $FUSE swap across Robinhood Chain DEX pairs plus protocol treasury RWA earnings are converted to tokenized stocks ($NVDA, $HOOD) and USDG, streaming directly to holders.

What is the collection allocation?

The collection consists of 3,300 devices for the public mint and 33 devices reserved for the core team & community giveaways.
